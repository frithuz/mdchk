# MD052: References without definitions

Status: analysis and configuration implemented for CommonMark and GFM. Findings
are report-only; there are no automatic fixes.

Area: [Links and images](index.md).

## Behavior

Recognize explicit full and collapsed reference syntax, including images: [text][id], [text][], ![text][id], ![text][]. Reuse the selected dialect reference-label grammar and normalization and authoritative definition collection. Bare [text] is not guessed to be an unresolved shortcut; shortcuts remain eligible only when positively identified as references by the parser. Exclude escaped/malformed bracket pairs and candidates overlapping another valid parsed construct.

Define bounded source-candidate recognizers alongside the selected dialect parser. Preserve parser ownership of valid constructs and protected code/raw/control regions. Do not infer author intent. Candidate recognition must not cross block/container boundaries; initially keep unparsed inline candidates to one physical line. Intentionally excluded ambiguous candidates are outside the rule scope and do not themselves cause incomplete coverage. Unreliable parsing or an unavailable required prerequisite still causes affected incomplete coverage.

Only exact, unescaped ASCII delimiter bytes establish the candidate structure:
an optional `!`, a flat `[text]` with no nested or unmatched brackets, and an
immediately adjacent `[]` (collapsed) or flat `[id]` (full). Escaped and
entity-decoded brackets never act as delimiters. The lookup label is the raw
original spelling of `id`, or of `text` for a collapsed candidate. It must meet
the parser's label grammar: no unescaped `[`, at most 999 characters with an
escape counted as two, and at least one character other than space or tab. It
is matched with the parser's Unicode case folding and whitespace collapse
against the completed definition collection, wherever the definition lives.

The whole candidate stays inside one contiguous text island on one physical
line, so link text containing emphasis, code spans, links or inline HTML is
excluded. Whole parsed links and images are opaque, including link text and
image alternatives. Code, HTML blocks, raw HTML lifetimes and tails, recognized
frontmatter and metadata are never scanned. Chains are excluded rather than
guessed: a candidate immediately preceded by `]` or `)`, starting right after a
parsed link, image or autolink, or immediately followed by `[` or `(` produces
no finding. An escaped `\!` makes the candidate a link candidate that starts at
its first `[`.

Each finding covers the complete original candidate, from `!` or the first `[`
through the final `]`, and says `Reference has no matching definition.`
Coordinates use original bytes and Unicode scalar columns, and never include a
line ending. A real suppressed finding counts as used for MD087.

Unavailable definitions, or unknown necessary source mapping or raw context,
withhold the entire inventory. The coverage message is
`Reference-definition context is unavailable; the complete MD052 inventory is withheld.`
Intentionally excluded syntax produces complete analysis without a finding.

See the [candidate contract](../../engineering/rule-behavior.md#malformed-syntax-candidates).

## Configuration

Default enablement: Enabled.

No rule-specific options.

All rules also accept the [shared settings](../index.md#configuration-and-findings).

## Examples

Violating input:

````markdown
[Guide][manual]
````

Accepted input:

````markdown
[Guide][manual]

[manual]: guide.md
````

Angle-bracket placeholders such as `<SPACE>`, `<TAB>`, `<EOF>`, and `<NEWLINE>` describe source characters rather than literal Markdown. Prose examples describe cases that cannot be represented directly in a UTF-8 document.

## Correction policy

No MD052 edits, replacement documents or deferred fix proposals are produced.

Report only; do not invent destinations or reinterpret ordinary brackets.

Any correction must satisfy the [shared preservation contract](../index.md#implementation-contract). Report remaining findings after checking the corrected document again.

## Validation

Cover the examples, every option, protected content, nested containers, LF and CRLF input, and precise diagnostic ranges. For corrections, verify preserved meaning and a second fix run with no further changes.
