# MD037: Whitespace within emphasis delimiters

Status: planned for implementation.

Area: [Formatting](index.md).

## Behavior

Recognize a single unambiguous, unnested pair of equal * or _ runs of length one or two in eligible inline text, with nonblank content and whitespace immediately inside at least one edge. Use word-boundary exclusions to avoid intraword marker runs; exclude escaped markers, code, competing/nested delimiter pairings and parser-owned list/thematic-break markers. Example source fixture: A * text * example. Do not treat the leading bullet in * text * at the start of a block as an emphasis opener.

Define bounded source-candidate recognizers alongside the selected dialect parser. Preserve parser ownership of valid constructs and protected code/raw/control regions. Do not infer author intent. Candidate recognition must not cross block/container boundaries; initially keep unparsed inline candidates to one physical line. Intentionally excluded ambiguous candidates are outside the rule scope and do not themselves cause incomplete coverage. Unreliable parsing or an unavailable required prerequisite still causes affected incomplete coverage.

See the [candidate contract](../../engineering/rule-behavior.md#malformed-syntax-candidates).

## Configuration

Default enablement: Enabled.

No rule-specific options.

All rules also accept the [shared settings](../index.md#configuration-and-findings). The defaults here are the planned mdchk contract.

## Examples

Violating input:

````markdown
A * text * example.
````

Accepted input:

````markdown
A *text* example.
````

Angle-bracket placeholders such as `<SPACE>`, `<TAB>`, `<EOF>`, and `<NEWLINE>` describe source characters rather than literal Markdown. Prose examples describe cases that cannot be represented directly in a UTF-8 document.

## Correction policy

Report only; removing spaces can introduce formatting that was not intended.

Any correction must satisfy the [shared preservation contract](../index.md#implementation-contract). Report remaining findings after checking the corrected document again.

## Validation

Cover the examples, every option, protected content, nested containers, LF and CRLF input, and precise diagnostic ranges. For corrections, verify preserved meaning and a second fix run with no further changes.
